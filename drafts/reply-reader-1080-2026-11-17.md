Hi, yes please, we would love that!

In the iOS app, tap "Add a tree" on Discover, take a photo standing by the tree, and send it. Your phone's location puts it on the map, and anything you know about it (a name, how old people say it is) goes with it. You can also do it on the website, with "Add a tree" at ancienttrees.app. Android app is coming soon.

We look at every tree that comes in, and the good ones go on the map.
