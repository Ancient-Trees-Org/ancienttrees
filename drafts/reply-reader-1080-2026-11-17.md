Hi, yes please, we would love that!

In the app, tap "Add a tree" on Discover, take a photo standing by the tree, and send it. Your phone's location puts it on the map, and anything you know about it (a name, how old people say it is) goes with it. You can also do it on the website at ancienttrees.app/contribute.

We look at every tree that comes in, and the good ones go on the map.
