They write: they do not live there so cannot be the ambassador; they ask whether there is a standard height above the ground for girth; they are happy with the site.

Nothing to change on the site: the ambassador invitation simply lapses. Our add form asks for girth "at chest height".

Suggested answer:

Thank you, that is lovely to hear, and no worries about the ambassador part.

On girth: measure round the trunk at about chest height, which most tree registers set at 1.3 metres above the ground (some in Britain use 1.5). On a slope, measure from the uphill side. If the trunk bulges or splits at that height, measure at the narrowest point below it and tell us the height you used. Any of that is welcome, the number with how you took it.
