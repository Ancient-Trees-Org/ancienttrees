# Reply to report 1000126 (The Great Pine of Romanya, gir_004)

Their words (the "dead or gone" chip, then): "Could only find a stump which suggests that a large tree used to be there. Pretty sure I was in the right location."

What we found: at first nothing written either way (the Catalan register still lists the pine, no news of a fall), so the thirteenth reader-judge run of 2026-11-22 kept the tree and asked for a photograph. Later the same day a reader's photograph came in through the app (sighting 3771a756, 7 m from our pin): cut sections of a large trunk on a mound, a holm oak beside it, open sky where a 30 m pine would stand. That is the stump photograph the rule asks for, so the tree was retired the same day (`retire.py girona gir_004`, kept in data/leads/girona.json as blocked, the page redirects). No standard mail went because they wrote words; this draft carries the thanks.
