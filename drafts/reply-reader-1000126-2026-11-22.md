# Reply to report 1000126 (The Great Pine of Romanya, gir_004)

Their words (the "dead or gone" chip, then): "Could only find a stump which suggests that a large tree used to be there. Pretty sure I was in the right location."

What we found: nothing written either way. The Catalan register still lists the pine (Pi Gros d'en Cama, declared 1991, condition normal, "330 m south of Romanyà de la Selva"), and a search found no news of it falling or being cut. One account without a photograph is not yet the evidence the rule asks for (a photograph of the stump, a second report, a news item or a council notice), so the page stays up, the report is closed `holds`, and the tree carries a note of the report in `verify_notes`. A photograph of the stump with its location takes the tree off the same day (`tree_reports.py --thank dead` is not right here because they wrote words; this draft carries the thanks).

Suggested answer:

> Thank you for going to look for the Great Pine of Romanya, and for telling us what you found. We could not find any news that it has come down, and the Catalan register still lists it, so for now it stays on the map. If you took a photograph of the stump, or still can, could you send it to us with where it stands? Then we can take the tree off the map the same day.
>
